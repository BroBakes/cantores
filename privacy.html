Privacy Policy.

How the Cantores Sancti Marci choir collects, uses, and protects your personal data — in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Effective 6 July 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what personal data we collect through this website, why we process it, and what rights you have in relation to it.

01Data Controller

ZBOR „CANTORES SANCTI MARCI"

Business ID (OIB): 99029223761

Registered address: Trg sv. Marka 5, 10000 Zagreb, Croatia

Email: cantores.sancti.marci@gmail.com

For any questions regarding the processing of personal data or to exercise your rights, please contact us at the email address above.

02What Data We Collect

Newsletter subscription

If you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect the email address you provide. We do not request or require any other information.

Contact by email

When you write to us, we process the data you send us (name, email address, and the content of your message) solely to respond to your enquiry.

Technical data

For security and to operate the website, our hosting provider may automatically log technical data such as IP address, browser type, and access times. We do not use this data to identify individuals.

03Purpose and Legal Basis

  • Newsletter: sending updates about concerts and the ensemble's work — based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you may withdraw at any time.
  • Responding to enquiries: communication at your request — based on legitimate interest and taking steps at your request (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f)).
  • Website security and operation: technical logs — based on our legitimate interest in the secure functioning of the site (Art. 6(1)(f)).

04Cookies and Analytics

This website does not use tracking cookies or any analytics or advertising tools. We do not build visitor profiles or track your behaviour on the site.

External resources

To display its typography, the site uses web fonts served from our own domain. We do not use external services (such as Google Fonts, analytics, or advertising) that would collect your data or transfer it to third parties.

05Retention Period

  • Newsletter email address: until you withdraw consent or unsubscribe.
  • Contact messages: for as long as needed to handle your enquiry, and then for no more than 12 months.
  • Technical logs: in accordance with our hosting provider's policy, generally for a short period.

06Data Sharing

We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for marketing purposes. Data may be processed by our processors (e.g. the hosting provider or a newsletter delivery service) solely on our behalf and under our instructions, subject to appropriate data processing agreements. We may disclose data to competent authorities where required by law.

07Your Rights

Under the GDPR, in relation to your personal data you have the right to:

  • access your data and information about its processing;
  • rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • erasure (the "right to be forgotten");
  • restriction of processing;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • data portability;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

You can send your request to cantores.sancti.marci@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority — the Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency (AZOP), Selska cesta 136, 10000 Zagreb (azop.hr).

08Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our activities or the law. The current version is always published on this page, with the effective date indicated.
